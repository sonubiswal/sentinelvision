The pipeline

Five deterministic stages, one accountable event

Detection is deliberately permissive, while commitment is deliberately conservative. Nothing is written to the audit log until the pipeline is sure it is looking at a person, and not at a flicker.

Policy & classes
Ten classes, each with its own confidence bar
Violations are derived, never assumed: a worker wearing the required item is compliant, and the matching absence class at a higher threshold records the finding. Context classes describe the scene and raise nothing on their own.


Positive PPEViolationContext class




Asymmetric by design. A violation class sits at 0.65 while its positive counterpart sits at 0.50, so raising an alarm always demands more evidence than recording compliance. Combined with the six-of-ten quorum, that ordering keeps a false positive off a supervisor's desk.
